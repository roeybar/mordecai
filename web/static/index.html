      About mordecai

      A research toolkit for resolving homographs with typed frames.

      A word like שערה can be hair or storm. mordecai finds the candidate readings of each word, and a solver picks one reading per word from the words around it. The chosen senses are then passed to the provider that renders the translation.

      Under every result you can see each word's candidates, the solver's pick, and a greedy baseline that always takes the first candidate.
